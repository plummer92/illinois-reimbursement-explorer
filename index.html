How estimates should work

Reimbursement Logic

Professional claim

Use CPT or HCPCS code, payer, provider type, modifier, locality, and fee schedule effective date.

Hospital outpatient

Use CPT/HCPCS, revenue code, APC/EAPG logic where available, drug J-code or NDC, and facility setting.

Hospital inpatient

Use diagnosis, procedures, APR-DRG or MS-DRG, length of stay, wage index, add-ons, and facility-specific policy.

Nursing facility

Use facility-level per diem rate, effective date, care category, Medicaid utilization, staffing add-ons, and quality incentives.

Actual patient responsibility needs benefit design data, including deductible, coinsurance, copay, network status, and prior authorization rules.
